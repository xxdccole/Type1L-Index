### Four-axis taxonomy and takeaways

#### Cement and binder families
- binder-portland — Binder · Portland I–V and air-entraining: Seeded: Types I, II, III, V and IA/IIA/IIIA; Type IV retained as historical low-heat classification, not implied current local availability. R28 now describes four current basic types. Verify adopted edition before specifying. Sources: R28, R140.
- binder-blended — Binder · IL / IS / IP / ternary and performance cements: Seeded: preserve IL research and add slag/pozzolan/ternary and C1157 performance route. C595-26 Type IC and changing composition require edition-specific checks; blend labels are not universal performance guarantees. Sources: R28, R37, R39, R40, R138, R140.
- binder-international — Binder · International CEM I–VI and regional classifications: Catalogue-level seed only: CEM I–V common cements are associated with EN 197-1; CEM II/C-M and CEM VI with EN 197-5.
