Binder · Portland I–V and air-entraining

9 resources · Last updated November 10, 2026

Key points

  • Seeded: Types I, II, III, V and IA/IIA/IIIA; Type IV retained as historical low-heat classification, not implied current local availability. R28 now describes four current basic types. Verify adopted edition before specifying.
  • Pass 2 (2026-11-09) historical backfill: NBS Lerch & Bogue 1934 heat of hydration and Flint & Wells 1941 hydrogarnet/sulfate work, Taylor's 1989 Bogue modification, NISTIR 4785 reference-clinker XRD and a 2022 history review (R210, R211, R212, R213, R214). Historic data explain Type II/IV/V logic; current ASTM C150 limits still govern.
