Key points

  • Seeded: Types I, II, III, V and IA/IIA/IIIA; Type IV retained as historical low-heat classification, not implied current local availability. R28 now describes four current basic types. Verify adopted edition before specifying.
  • Pass 2 (2026-11-09) historical backfill: NBS Lerch & Bogue 1934 heat of hydration and Flint & Wells 1941 hydrogarnet/sulfate work, Taylor's 1989 Bogue modification, NISTIR 4785 reference-clinker XRD and a 2022 history review (R210, R211, R212, R213, R214). Historic data explain Type II/IV/V logic; current ASTM C150 limits still govern.
  • Nov 13 backfill (DOI/abstract level): Portland clinker and cement classics — Shepherd & Rankin 1911 ternary CaO–Al2O3–SiO2 system, 1960s–70s clinker microscopy (prismatic/interstitial phases, SEM, XRD minor phases), alkalies in cement (Jawed & Skalny 1977), sulfate-resisting cement (Gollop & Taylor 1994/1995), low-heat Portland cement (incl. 2023 review), alite/belite and alite–belite–ye'elimite cements, early air-entrainment papers (Powers 1954 void spacing, 1946–1959 ACI) (R1893–R2561, 92 records).
  • Nov 22 backfill: clinker-phase doping (Mg, Zn), early setting/hardening studies back to 1919 and strength-development models for blended cement mortars (R4323–R4563, 35 records). DOI/abstract level only.
