SCM / addition · Calcined clay / metakaolin

21 resources · Last updated November 10, 2026

Key points

  • Foundational seed: Calcined clay is an SCM; LC3 is a blended binder system using it. Clay quality and process energy are important.
  • Pass 3: metakaolin foundations (Wild 1996; Frías 2000; Boddy 2001; Sabir 2001 review; Greek kaolins 2004), clay-mineral pozzolanicity (2011), metakaolin+limestone (2012), R3 test development (2016) and RILEM TC 267/TC 282 reviews, LC3 factors, 2026 clinker×clay mineralogy study and VDOT VTRC 26-R52 field-agency evaluation of LC3/LC2 vs fly ash (R258, R261, R262, R263, R264, R265, R266, R267, R268, R269, R270, R271, R272, R273, R274, R358). ASTM C1897 text itself not reviewed.
