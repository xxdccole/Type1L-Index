9 resources · Last updated November 10, 2026

Key points

Type IL collection. These points describe portland-limestone (Type IL) mixtures and exposures already indexed. They are not general rules for every cement or concrete.

  • Partly covered: shotcrete (R55); 3D printing now three lab studies (R56, R57, R132) — R132 also gives retail PLC/limestone unit costs in a TEA context.
  • Partly covered: SCC (R72); mass concrete lab/modeling (R105–R124, R128) but no Type IL field pour with temperature monitoring.
  • Still open: Type IL ready-mix market cost from a reliable industry source; primary ACI 318/301 code text (R138 is secondary); EN 197-1 official text (only secondary citations).
  • Microcracking/ITZ imaging in commercial Type IL vs OPC concrete still missing.
