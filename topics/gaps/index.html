Key points

  • Field performance data: Lab-to-field correlation and performance-based specs still developing.
  • Partly covered now: Shotcrete (one 2026 ACI study) and 3D printing (two lab studies).
  • Not yet covered by found sources: Mass concrete, SCC, and cost data specific to Type IL; primary ACI 318/301 code text.
  • SCC and mass concrete now have one source each: a 2018 Georgia Tech dissertation on limestone-blended SCC (R72) and a 2017 field study of hydration heat in a mass floor with limestone blended cement and fly ash (R73). Cost data remains a gap.
