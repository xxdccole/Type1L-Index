Concrete · Heavyweight / shielding / ballast

45 resources · Last updated November 12, 2026

Key points

  • Seeded / partial: Density and form pressure/handling require special consideration; shielding design outside reviewed source.
  • Pass 2: gamma and neutron shielding evidence (barite/magnetite/goethite/serpentine HPC; 2021 neutron and 2024 radiation-shielding reviews; ferrophosphorus weighting for well cement) (R221, R222, R223, R224). Lab/review evidence; shielding design requires project-specific nuclear analysis and ACI 349/NRC-type requirements not reviewed.
