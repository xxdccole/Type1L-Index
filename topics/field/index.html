Field issues and finishing

27 resources · Last updated November 8, 2026

Key points

Type IL collection. These points describe portland-limestone (Type IL) mixtures and exposures already indexed. They are not general rules for every cement or concrete.

  • Bleed and finish: Type IL often bleeds slower; premature finish and water on the surface raise blistering/delamination risk (ASCC Field Guide R10; Euclid contractor guide R133).
  • WDM forensic (R135): West Des Moines Type IL pavements with scaling/low strength — nothing wrong with Type IL itself; distress tied to high/variable air, mixing, late retempering, finishing and curing responding to different bleed/set. Target ~5–8% air; unit weight at discharge; trial batches/test strips; enforce curing.
  • Curing and evaporation control matter more when bleeding is reduced (Euclid R93, R133; PACA Oct 2026).
  • Crystalline admixtures/sealers are being marketed with Type IL for freeze-thaw and chloride resistance (Concrete Products R134; vendor-authored — treat as practice note, not independent research).
  • Preconstruction evaluation and producer collaboration remain best practice when cement source changes.
