Field issues and finishing

13 resources · Last updated November 4, 2026

Key points

  • Symptoms: Crusting, differential set, crazing, delamination, plastic shrinkage cracking, low breaks.
  • Remedies: Control set (accelerators), evaporation retarder or fogging at 0.05-0.08 lb/ft2/hr, flat first trowel, no early sealing, no over-troweling.
  • A March 2026 two-hour WACA training deck covers placing and finishing Type IL concrete (R71).
  • Fall 2026 trade guidance converges on curing and documentation: Euclid (R93) says Type IL mixes need mixture-specific curing decisions, PACA (R95) ties recent winter durability claims to fall placements with poor finishing and curing, and S3 (R96) tells flooring installers to get the mill certificate, cure product and finish method before bidding.
