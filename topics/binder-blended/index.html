Key points

  • Seeded: preserve IL research and add slag/pozzolan/ternary and C1157 performance route. C595-26 Type IC and changing composition require edition-specific checks; blend labels are not universal performance guarantees.
  • Pass 3 (2026-11-10): NBS 1976 energy case for blended cements, early ternary-blend durability/strength papers (1997–2007), SCM reactivity review (2019), CO2-reduction techniques (2016), low-clinker systems (2023), RILEM pore-solution/pore-structure and TC 282 mixture-design reviews (R253, R254, R255, R256, R257, R258, R259, R260, R264, R273, R317, R332, R333, R358). Mostly DOI/abstract-level; US C595 Type IP/IS/IT history beyond R37/R39/R40 still thin.
