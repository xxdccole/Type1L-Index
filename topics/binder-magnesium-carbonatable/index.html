Binder · Magnesium-based and carbonatable binders

77 resources · Last updated November 13, 2026

Key points

  • Reviews distinguish Mg silicate/carbonate/oxysalt and carbonatable calcium-silicate systems. CO2 curing differs from injecting CO2 into normal concrete; steel passivation, water resistance, energy and carbon accounting remain limitations.
  • Deepened 2026-11-08: Walling/Provis Chemical Reviews DOI (WRRO landing verified) and Juenger et al. alternative-binders review. Low-pH steel limits remain explicit.
  • Nov 12 backfill: Sorel/magnesium oxychloride cement (Tooper & Cartz 1966 Nature; Ball 1977; Deng 2003; Li & Chau 2007), magnesium phosphate rapid repair (Seehra et al. 1993), carbonation curing of precast and a 2026 MOC review (R1655–R1795, 10 records). DOI/abstract level.
