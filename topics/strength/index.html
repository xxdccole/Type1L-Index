Strength, curing, cold weather

16 resources · Last updated November 6, 2026

Key points

  • Similar 28-day strength: Some field reports of lower early strength and more weather sensitivity.
  • Type IL(HE): High-early PLC replacing Type III in precast and cold weather.
  • Cold weather: Standard ACI 306 practice applies; protect until about 500 psi.
  • Same-clinker PLC can match OPC early (<24 h) compressive strength in HES mortars with set accelerator; SCMs may slow early strength and should be limited based on SCM reactivity tests (R126).
