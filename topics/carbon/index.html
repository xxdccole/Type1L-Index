Carbon, GWP and EPDs

14 resources · Last updated November 6, 2026

Key points

  • About 8% lower GWP: Industry EPDs: PLC (10.8% limestone) is 8.2% lower than portland cement; FHWA cites 8.3%; ACI up to 10%.
  • Grinding: Extra grinding energy is outweighed by clinker reduction.
  • New PCR (Oct 2026): ACA is surveying U.S. producers for updated industry-average EPDs, including portland-limestone cement.
  • Plant-specific Type IL EPDs keep appearing, e.g. Amrize Devils Slide IL(10), issued Feb 2026 (R68).
  • NRMCA released its Industry-Wide LCA and EPD Version 4 in October 2026 (R99, R100), with PLC included in the modeled cement supply.
  • Low-dosage (0.3–0.6%) colloidal nanosilica can boost CO2 uptake in Type IL paste; OPC still carbonates more readily, while nanosilica aids PLC via air stabilization and available CH (R127).
  • ACA blended-cements hub (R138): PLC use since 2021 credited with avoiding over 13.3 Mt CO2; industry-average PLC ~8.2% lower CO2 vs PC per EPDs.
  • CP Tech/FHWA paving carbon interim guide (R137): Type IL typically ~8–10% lower cradle-to-gate GWP if cement content is not increased; do not raise cementitious content to compensate.
