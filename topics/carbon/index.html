Carbon, GWP and EPDs

8 resources · Last updated October 30, 2026

Key points

  • About 8% lower GWP: Industry EPDs: PLC (10.8% limestone) is 8.2% lower than portland cement; FHWA cites 8.3%; ACI up to 10%.
  • Grinding: Extra grinding energy is outweighed by clinker reduction.
  • New PCR (Oct 2026): ACA is surveying U.S. producers for updated industry-average EPDs, including portland-limestone cement.
  • Plant-specific Type IL EPDs keep appearing, e.g. Amrize Devils Slide IL(10), issued Feb 2026 (R68).
