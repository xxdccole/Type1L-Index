Binder · Rapid-set / expansive / shrinkage-compensating

47 resources · Last updated November 10, 2026

Key points

  • Foundational preview discusses rapid-hardening and controlled early expansion. Rapid set is not synonymous with high early strength; shrinkage compensation requires restraint and curing design. Current C845/C1600 requirements not verified.
  • Deepened 2026-11-08: ACI PRC-223-21 preview plus ASTM C845 expansive and C1600 rapid-hardening catalogue records. Expansion needs restraint/curing design; rapid-hardening types are performance classes, not a single chemistry.
