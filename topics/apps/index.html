Precast, floors, bridges

26 resources · Last updated November 11, 2026

Key points

Type IL collection. These points describe portland-limestone (Type IL) mixtures and exposures already indexed. They are not general rules for every cement or concrete.

  • Precast and prestressed: PCI Journal 2025 and USDOT research: IL usable in girders; prestress loss and transfer length favorable.
  • Industrial floors: Comparable abrasion with good finishing and curing.
  • Tilt-up: Bond breaker compatibility concerns raised with C595-26 compositions.
  • Shotcrete: ACI Materials Journal 2026 study found PLC wet-mix shotcrete needs more alkali-free accelerator for early strength, with similar 7- and 28-day strength and fiber toughness to portland cement.
  • 3D printing: Early studies use Type IL / GULb binders with cellulose nanomaterials or quarry fines to meet printability.
  • Type IL is being used in UHPC with limestone-enriched binders and low-grade calcined clay (R129) and in architectural white concrete (IU Ferguson, ~11% limestone PLC; R130).
  • 3D printing (R132, Nature Comm 2026): PLC with additional limestone filler (to ~29–40% LF vs OPC) plus 0.3% cellulose nanofibers improved buildability of overhang geometries vs two commercial printable mixes; TEA/LCA report lower cost and GWP when strength is the functional unit.
