Mix design guidance

8 resources · Last updated November 10, 2026

Key points

Type IL collection. These points describe portland-limestone (Type IL) mixtures and exposures already indexed. They are not general rules for every cement or concrete.

  • 1:1 replacement: Keep cement content; run trial batches and verify fresh and hardened properties.
  • Adjust: Water within w/cm limit, admixture type and dosage, aggregate grading.
