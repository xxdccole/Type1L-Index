Key points

Type IL collection. These points describe portland-limestone (Type IL) mixtures and exposures already indexed. They are not general rules for every cement or concrete.

  • 1:1 replacement: Keep cement content; run trial batches and verify fresh and hardened properties.
  • Adjust: Water within w/cm limit, admixture type and dosage, aggregate grading.
  • Nov 21 backfill: high-volume fly-ash concretes (strength, durability, nano-silica, recycled aggregate), interfacial-transition-zone and low-w/c paste microstructure, mix design of fibre-reinforced SCC (R4216–R4222, 7 records). DOI/abstract level only.
  • Nov 22 open-queue: optimised/blended aggregate gradation reports (NDOR, Oklahoma State, FHWA, NCC), KCMMB 2026 mix specification and the 1996 TRR paper on high-dosage Type C fly ash with limestone (R4309–R4322, 8 records).
