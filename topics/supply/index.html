Supply, producers, adoption

12 resources · Last updated November 10, 2026

Key points

Type IL collection. These points describe portland-limestone (Type IL) mixtures and exposures already indexed. They are not general rules for every cement or concrete.

  • Market share: Blended cement about 5% of U.S. market in 2021; about 65 Mt vs 35 Mt portland in 2025 (ACA).
  • Producers: Examples seen: Amrize, Cemex, Heidelberg, GCC, St. Marys/Votorantim, Illinois Cement.
  • Variability: Switching IL sources is a recognized concern.
  • Source-to-source PLC variability is now quantified by FHWA (R125): chemical mill-report values track measured data better than physical (Blaine/density); batch-to-batch early heat of Type IL(10) from one plant varied ≤5%.
  • NCC Blended Cement Steering (R136): collective SHA request for Type IL plant data (strength, set, fineness, LOI, SO3, limestone) from 2022–mid-2025; analysis ongoing with peer review.
  • WVDOH Type 1L approved mills (R139) expand named-source QPL coverage beyond OKDOT IL(10) (R131).
