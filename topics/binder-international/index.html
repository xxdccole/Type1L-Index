67 resources · Last updated November 13, 2026

Key points

  • Catalogue-level seed only: CEM I–V common cements are associated with EN 197-1; CEM II/C-M and CEM VI with EN 197-5. Not one interchangeable CEM I–VI standard; full normative text and global crosswalk remain gaps.
  • Pass 2 / current: EN 197-6:2023 RCF cements (preview), Dutch EN 197 overview incl. NEN 8005/CUR 48 rule, Swiss SIA 215/1 (2025) and SN EN 206 ND (2025) paper, Polish CEM II/C-M & CEM VI concrete data (R206, R207, R208, R249). China GB 175 and India IS 269/1489 still not covered by verified sources.
