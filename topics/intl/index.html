Canada and Europe

20 resources · Last updated November 13, 2026

Key points

Type IL collection. These points describe portland-limestone (Type IL) mixtures and exposures already indexed. They are not general rules for every cement or concrete.

  • CSA A3001 GUL: Up to 15% limestone since 2008; same requirements as GU; Blaine typically about 100 m2/kg higher.
  • EN 197-1 CEM II/A-L, B-L: In use in Europe since the 1960s-2000s; may be lower strength class than CEM I, unlike U.S./Canadian equivalence.
  • GCCA: Treats limestone as a clinker substitute with synergies with other SCMs; flags clay and organic contamination as frost-durability risks.
  • Nature Comm printable concrete study (R132) cites EN 197-1 allowing up to 35 wt% limestone replacement (vs ASTM C595 Type IL ≤15%); study used PLC plus additional LF, not a commercial EN CEM II/B-L product alone.
