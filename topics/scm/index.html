Use with SCMs

19 resources · Last updated November 5, 2026

Key points

  • Synergy: Limestone with aluminous SCMs (fly ash, slag) forms carboaluminates, refining pores.
  • Ternary mixes: Common in U.S. practice; needed for severe sulfate exposure.
  • ACA's Sept 2026 contractor article (R97) lays out a five-step vetting workflow (supplier data, targeted testing, mortar screening, concrete testing, field mockups) for blended cements.
  • Low-grade calcined clay combined with limestone-enriched ASTM Type IL cement is under study for UHPC (R129).
