Binder · White / colored / masonry / mortar / plastic

46 resources · Last updated November 10, 2026

Key points

  • Seeded historical specialty classification and manufacturer white masonry page. Distinguish cement from mortar, pigment from binder chemistry, C91 masonry from C1329 mortar and C1328 plastic cement; latest normative clauses and independent comparisons remain gaps.
  • Pass 2: NBS Monograph 36 (1961) shows bond strength dominated wall racking/flexure; repair-mortar guidance for traditional masonry, white clinker defects and LBNL albedo data showing white-cement concrete markedly more reflective (R215, R216, R217, R218). Still no current ASTM C91/C1328/C1329 text reviewed.
