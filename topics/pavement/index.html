Key points

Type IL collection. These points describe portland-limestone (Type IL) mixtures and exposures already indexed. They are not general rules for every cement or concrete.

  • Type IL is widely used in paving; CP Tech and FHWA guides support 1:1 replacement with trial batching (R22, R25, R137).
  • WDM Iowa forensic (R135): early Type IL urban pavements showed scaling/abrasion and strength scatter linked to construction response (air, water, finish, cure), not an inherent cement defect; practice has reportedly improved.
  • Crystalline technologies claimed to aid IL pavement durability in Iowa case notes (R134).
