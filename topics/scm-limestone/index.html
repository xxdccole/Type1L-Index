138 resources · Last updated November 13, 2026

Key points

  • Foundational seed: Limestone filler is separately classified from pozzolanic SCMs; packing/nucleation and carboaluminate effects do not make every filler a pozzolan.
  • Pass 2: carboaluminate (2001), early filler-strength (1977), 2018 review and limestone-cement sulfate microstructure (2003) studies; also CEM II/C-M/CEM VI data (R209, R242, R243, R244, R245, R249). Mostly DOI-level metadata; full texts closed.
  • Nov 12 PLC/limestone backfill: 1988–1991 ASTM/CRC “carbonate additions” literature (Mayfield 1988; Livesey 1991; Barker & Cory 1991; Adams & Race 1990; Bédard & Bergeron 1990; Neto & Campiteli 1990), Gutteridge & Dalziel 1990 filler-cement papers, Tsivilis 1999 and Vuk 2001 PLC parameters, Purdue 2013 and Caltrans 2021 PLC reports, and Nov 2026 limestone/gypsum content determination (R1671–R1850, 32 records).
  • Nov 13 PLC/limestone backfill: forward citers of Tsivilis 1999 and the 1991 limestone-filled cement papers (De Weerdt et al. 2011 ternary limestone–fly ash; Ipavec et al. 2010 carboaluminates; Lothenbach 2010 thermodynamics; Torres et al. 2003 and Hartshorn et al. 2001 PLC mortars in magnesium sulfate), limestone filler sulfate-attack review (Irassar 2009), CEMHYD3D modelling, rheology/chemical shrinkage with filler, PLC concrete pavements and FE heat-of-hydration with limestone blended cement (R1905–R2532, 33 records).
