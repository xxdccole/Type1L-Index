73 resources · Last updated November 11, 2026

Key points

  • Foundational seed: Limestone filler is separately classified from pozzolanic SCMs; packing/nucleation and carboaluminate effects do not make every filler a pozzolan.
  • Pass 2: carboaluminate (2001), early filler-strength (1977), 2018 review and limestone-cement sulfate microstructure (2003) studies; also CEM II/C-M/CEM VI data (R209, R242, R243, R244, R245, R249). Mostly DOI-level metadata; full texts closed.
