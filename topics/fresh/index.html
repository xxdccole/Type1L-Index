Fresh properties and admixtures

12 resources · Last updated November 5, 2026

Key points

  • Higher fineness: Can raise water demand about 1-3 gal/yd3 and reduce bleeding.
  • Admixtures: Re-tune dosages; avoid over-dosing retarding water reducers; verify air entrainment per source.
  • Setting: Reports of unpredictable or differential set and top-down crusting in flatwork.
  • New ASCE JMCE study (R94) of Texas, Colorado and Florida IL cements: lower strength development than Type I/II, higher shrinkage in the first 24 h, but up to 35% higher surface and bulk resistivity; results depended on IL fineness, oxide composition and admixture dosage.
  • FHWA R125: PLC fineness (PSD more than Blaine) and sulfate balance drive early workability and set; ACI R126 shows set accelerators can support high-early-strength PLC mortars comparable to same-clinker OPC within 24 h.
